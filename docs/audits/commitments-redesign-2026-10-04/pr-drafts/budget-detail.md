Before: budget details relied on the older header, a collapsible chart and separate activity-filter controls. After: lead with remaining or over-limit spending, the shared status and period comparison; keep the current/previous chart visible; and make category rows filter Activity directly. Setup becomes one edit row and Add expense becomes a floating action.

The chart aligns current cumulative spending, even pace and the previous period. It supports refund-driven negative totals and hides unavailable comparisons or misleading pace text during loading, errors or incomplete FX. Activity keeps the shared journal cards, selection and pagination; the composer retains selected/sole-category prefill. Missing or deleted budgets remain handled. The budget name heads the page; Edit and confirmed Delete sit in the overflow menu beside privacy, with navigation waiting for native menu dismissal. Monetary text and accessible labels use privacy-aware formatting.

Validation: independent typecheck; 26 focused budget/menu tests covering native dismissal ordering, single-category hiding, filters, refunds, missing FX, privacy, exact totals and navigation. Recorded full-stack verification passed `bun run verify` with 477 suites / 3,010 tests, architecture/privacy checks, both typechecks, coverage and lint (one existing journal-suggestions warning); see [the exact verification record](https://github.com/SahilSoniOrg/full-frills-balance/blob/codex/commitments-redesign-planned-detail/docs/audits/commitments-redesign-2026-10-04/VERIFICATION.md).
