Before: the Budgets tab used the older tab and row layout, without a combined summary or the shared pace presentation. After: show a monthly summary and compact budget rows sorted by urgency, with left/over amounts, category chips, status-colored progress and a today marker. The segmented tabs retain route selection and display counts.

Summary policy: aggregate only one-month budgets in the workplace currency. Count other currencies and cadences separately; differing monthly reset dates suppress the shared period label and marker. Incomplete-FX review remains a separate action. The Budget floating action and existing form/navigation flows remain available. Amounts and accessible labels respect privacy, and rows remain readable at narrow widths and large text sizes.

Validation: independent typecheck; budget component and route-selection regression tests.
