Before: payment details led with disclosure sections and split actions. After: put the saved occurrence amount, due urgency, and paired Record / Skip actions first; follow with future dates, currency-grouped lifetime totals, recent history and schedule details. The payment name heads the page; Edit, Pause/Resume, pending-entry review and confirmed Delete move to the overflow menu.

Record and Skip retain confirmation, busy/disabled states, write locking, inline errors and stay-open behavior. History keeps selection, sharing and pagination. Same-currency differences show paid more/less labels; different-currency payments retain the original rule amount without an invented FX comparison. Paused schedules show no future projections and use only an audited pause timestamp. Ended schedules show lifetime totals and the last recorded month; outstanding occurrences remain actionable. Finite schedules show a remaining count only when it can be established exactly. All monetary text and accessible labels respect privacy.

Validation: independent typecheck; actual-component and presentation tests for edited amounts, urgency, history, currency, privacy, paused/ended states, errors and disabled actions, plus audited-date and finite-count helper tests. Recorded full-stack verification passed `bun run verify` with 477 suites / 3,010 tests, architecture/privacy checks, both typechecks, coverage and lint (one existing journal-suggestions warning); see [the exact verification record](https://github.com/SahilSoniOrg/full-frills-balance/blob/codex/commitments-redesign-planned-detail/docs/audits/commitments-redesign-2026-10-04/VERIFICATION.md).
