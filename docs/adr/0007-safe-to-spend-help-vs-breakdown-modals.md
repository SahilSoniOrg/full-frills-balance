# ADR-0007: Safe to Spend help teaches; breakdown detail audits

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-26

The dashboard exposes Safe to Spend through a **?** help entry and tappable **breakdown slices** (safe, reserved, outstanding). We keep two sheets: **Safe to Spend explanation** teaches how the headline is defined and calculated; **breakdown detail** shows what makes up one slice at the current workplace. The safe-slice breakdown does not repeat the full formula—only audit-oriented content (description, headline amount, upcoming income lines) plus a single CTA that **replaces** the breakdown sheet with the explanation sheet. Committed and outstanding breakdown sheets stay audit-only with no link to explanation.
