# Centralize launch orchestration above books providers

The root launch coordinator is the canonical owner of Device recovery, Workplace discovery, launch resolution, launch-time Active-workplace pointer repair, splash completion, route guarding, and interactive Workplace transitions. Creation and Import may persist the pointer for their newly committed Workplace as part of their completion flow; other callers must request a transition through the coordinator. Internal services may remain focused on their bounded persistence work. The pure resolver returns the exact target, including `open(workplaceId, persistAsActive)`; callers never rediscover it. Books-only providers and routes mount only for `open`, while ambiguous books deep links received before then are rejected. `WorkplaceProvider` receives the valid ID and never discovers or creates a Workplace. This prevents Device onboarding, Workplace creation, Import, picker, deep links, and splash hydration from independently inferring whether books are usable.
