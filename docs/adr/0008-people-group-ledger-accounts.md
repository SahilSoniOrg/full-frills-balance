# People group ordinary ledger accounts instead of parenting them

**Status:** Accepted

People is a Workplace-level grouping and workflow over ordinary ledger Accounts, not a cross-type parent Account or synthetic posting account. Each Person can group linked Asset, Liability, Income, and Expense accounts across currencies; this preserves the existing same-type account-tree rule and double-entry journal model while enabling a person-focused history and balance view. See [FUL-97](https://linear.app/full-frills-balance/issue/FUL-97/people-books-person-open-items-splits-and-settle-up-in-a-workplace) for the behavior contract.
