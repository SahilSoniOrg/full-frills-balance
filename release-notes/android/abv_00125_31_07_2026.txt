New release 🚀

Safer money moves, clearer Safe to Spend, and a guided way to split one purchase across categories — plus daily budgets and stronger privacy.

---

Also in this update:

* Split Journal Entry: Log one expense across several categories in a guided flow, with shared description, date, and notes across simple, split, and advanced modes.
* Classify Balance Changes: When you update an account balance, choose where the difference goes — a counterparty account or an adjustment — instead of it always landing in Balance Corrections.
* Daily Budgets: Set a budget that renews every day from the budget editor.
* Clearer Safe to Spend: Friendlier wording when you’re short of your plan, and a Settings toggle to show or hide the Safe to Spend chart.
* Safer Imports: A failed restore no longer wipes your workplace; unbalanced backups are rejected before they can replace your data.
* Account Protections: You can’t delete an account that still has transactions, and account cards show clearer period stats and icons.
* Multi-Currency Consistency: Balances, reports, budgets, and wealth totals convert more consistently when you use more than one currency.

---

Big difference you’ll notice:

* Splitting a grocery run or shared bill across categories feels intentional instead of fiddly.
* Editing an account balance asks you to classify the change before it saves.
* Daily spending limits and a quieter or richer Safe to Spend card are yours to choose.
* Restoring a backup feels safer — a bad file won’t erase what you already have.

---

Under the hood, we rebuilt large parts of the ledger and reporting layers for clearer ownership, added native end-to-end test coverage, tightened privacy and currency conversion paths, and sped up guided journal entry and post-import balance rebuilds.
