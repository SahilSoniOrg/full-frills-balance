New release 🚀

Card purchases now show a red minus and repayments show a green plus in your card activity, totals and charts. The app also retries interrupted balance recalculations automatically.

---

Also in this update

* Popups — The main button is easier to spot, and the appearance picker opens over the screen you're on.
* Account edits — You can enter a negative balance for an overdrawn account or an overpaid card.
* Gmail — Removed for now.

---

Big difference you'll notice

* If you overpay a card, Safe to Spend applies that credit to spending on the same card when calculating upcoming payments.
