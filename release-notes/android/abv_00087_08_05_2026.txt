New release 🚀

This one is a pretty big foundational update.

You can now use multiple Workplaces inside the app.

Think of them like separate books:

* one for personal finances
* one for business
* one for anything else you want separated

This is also the foundation for future features like:

* multi-device sync
* shared workplaces
* view-only access
* collaborative bookkeeping

The app will still stay local-first.
Online sync will be optional.

---

Also in this update:

* redesigned account creation flow
* redesigned journal creation + journal details pages
* redesigned settings page
* added notes support in journals
* backups are now compressed ZIP files
* exports are smaller + cleaner
* added bug reporting in Settings
* bunch of bug fixes + stability improvements

---

A lot of work in this release went into making the app more reliable internally and preparing the base for much larger features coming next.
