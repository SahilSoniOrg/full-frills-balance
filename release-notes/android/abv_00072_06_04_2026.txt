New release 🚀

Know exactly which account will run dry — before it happens.

We’ve added account-level Safe to Spend.

Now you can see how much you can safely spend from each account, not just overall — so you don’t end up with money in one place and a shortage in another.

What’s new

- Per-account Safe to Spend (inside the dashboard breakdown)
- Credit card payments → choose which account will pay it
- Budgets → assign which accounts they spend from

Why this matters

You get a much clearer picture of:

- which account needs money
- where your spending is actually coming from
- what might break before your next income

Less guessing. Fewer surprises.
