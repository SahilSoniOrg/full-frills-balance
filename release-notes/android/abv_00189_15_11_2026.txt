New release 🚀

SMS capture works differently. The old auto-post rules are gone, replaced by a switch you turn on for each workplace, and it only picks up messages that arrive after you turn it on. A captured message can also settle a planned payment instead of adding a second entry.

---

Also in this update:

* Planned payments: A message that matches a scheduled payment settles it, even with scheduled posting off. Near matches wait in the inbox.
* Names: Activity is now Transactions, notification settings are Spending reminders, and the SMS screen is Transaction review.
* Gmail: Built, but Google approval is still pending, so it stays off for now.
* Web: The app runs in a browser at fullfrillsapp.sahilsoni.in. It keeps its own data and doesn't sync with your phone yet.
* Merchant names: UPI and statement messages keep the full name instead of cutting it short.
* Setup: First-time setup now saves your progress, so you can stop and resume.

---

Big difference you'll notice:

* A captured message posts as Uncategorized and waits for you to pick a category. Anything the app can't read stays in the inbox.

---

Under the hood, SMS and email share one capture path, and restored backups start with capture off.
