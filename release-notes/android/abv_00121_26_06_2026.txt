New release 🚀

A focused update on stability, performance, and under-the-hood improvements.

---

Also in this update:

* Reactive Journal Updates: When you add, edit, or delete an account, the journal view now updates automatically without requiring a manual refresh.
* Smarter SMS Rules: SMS rule management has been split into focused sub-components — match mode selection, recent matches preview, and a rule flow preview — making the setup experience cleaner and easier to follow.
* Faster Startup: The mock data seeder now lazy-loads, cutting unnecessary work at app startup for a snappier first open.
* Selection Toolbar Improvements: Cleaner selection UX across list views.
* Safer Navigation: Navigation helpers now use proper type-safe routes, catching broken links at build time instead of runtime.

---

Under the hood, we refactored the SMS rule engine into a dedicated repository and parser, added web platform shims for Metro, and improved test coverage across journal, import, and audit flows.
