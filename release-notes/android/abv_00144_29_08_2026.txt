New release 🚀

Choose how times appear throughout the app, add notes to individual rows in bulk journal entries, and move through onboarding more smoothly. To change the new display options, open Settings > Appearance & Display.

---

Also in this update:

* Clock format: Under Time, choose System, 12-hour, or 24-hour.
* Bulk journal notes: Add an optional note to each row while entering several transactions at once.
* Account pickers: Turn on Compact account picker under Display Options to see more accounts at once.
* Account appearance: Choose from more icons and colors, with imported colors and account settings kept intact.
* Safer account cleanup: Merging accounts now preserves linked journals, budgets, planned payments, and other account details.
* More accurate balances: Historical account transactions are counted correctly.
* Clearer journal entries: Exchange rates are easier to read, and imported bank messages no longer fill journal notes with the original SMS text.
* Smoother screens: Onboarding, bottom navigation, time pickers, and inline search controls behave more reliably.

---

Big difference you’ll notice:

* Dates and times now use your chosen clock format consistently across the app.

Under the hood, account merges and journal updates now complete more safely and consistently.
