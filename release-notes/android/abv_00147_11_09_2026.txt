New release 🚀

This update fixes a few frustrating rough edges. Entering long amounts no longer clips off digits, setting up a new workplace flows smoothly, and reports update right away with cleaner category charts.

---

Also in this update:

* Amount fields: Long numbers scale down to fit comfortably so digits don't get cut off.
* Workplace setup: Creating a workplace now moves through each step reliably and remembers where you left off.
* Live reports: Reports update the moment you add a transaction, keeping income and expense categories strictly separate.
* Account organizing: Nesting or moving accounts updates parent totals right away.
* Parent account picker: Only eligible accounts show up when choosing a parent for a subaccount.
* SMS rules: Cleaned up duplicate merchant suggestions in settings.

---

Big difference you’ll notice:

* You can type out large amounts without numbers getting cut off, and creating a new workplace works without getting stuck.

---

Under the hood, report calculations and account tree updates are more reliable and run with fewer intermediate steps.
