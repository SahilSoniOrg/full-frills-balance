New release 🚀

This release gives the app a more consistent feel. Icons now match across screens, the wording is clearer and more natural, and calculations handle invalid inputs more safely.

---

Also in this update:

* Icons: Common actions and account types use the same visual language throughout the app.
* Copy and tone: Labels, messages, and guidance have been rewritten to feel clearer and less technical.
* Safer calculations: Invalid simulation inputs are rejected instead of producing misleading results.
* Workplace details: Renaming a workplace or changing its icon refreshes right away.
* Appearance: Typeface changes apply reliably when you switch them.
* Bug fixes: Small restore-related issues have been fixed.

---

Big difference you’ll notice:

* The app feels more coherent from screen to screen, with familiar icons and friendlier language.

---

Under the hood, shared boundaries and validation now keep the app’s behavior more consistent.
