Prescriptions

Critical Accessibility labels on icon-only controls. Info buttons, settings cog, close X, and search icon are silent to TalkBack/VoiceOver. Add accessibilityLabel to AppIcon and IconButton.
High Reduced motion support. Disable skeleton pulse and entrance animations when prefers-reduced-motion is active. Add AccessibilityInfo / useReducedMotion hook.
High Color-blind safe coding. Red/green income-expense pairing is the most common color vision deficiency. Add shape/underline/badge indicators alongside color.
Medium Dark mode weight compensation. Bump body text weight by one step (regular → medium, medium → semibold) in dark mode to counteract optical thinning of light-on-dark text.
Medium Tablet/landscape layout adaptation. Recompose hero chart and breakdown bar side-by-side on wider screens. Increase maxContentWidth for tablets. Support landscape orientation.
Low Gold Obsidian contrast audit. Verify gold (#D4AF37) on dark surface (#16181D) passes WCAG AA for body text (4.5:1).
Low Box memo dependency audit. Reduce 30+ dependency array in Box useMemo to prevent invalidation in hot render paths.
